Our Story

Built by founders who were tired of building alone

2021

It started with a group text. Three e-commerce founders—strangers who'd met at a Shopify meetup in the Seaport—venting about the same problems: isolation, imposter syndrome, and the feeling that everyone else had it figured out.

We decided to grab coffee the next week. That coffee turned into a monthly dinner. The dinner turned into a dozen founders. And suddenly, we had something real.

2022

We realized the magic wasn't in the tactics we shared—it was in the permission to be honest. About struggling months. About imposter syndrome. About the gap between Instagram success stories and late-night anxiety.

So we formalized it. Created a real application process. Set revenue requirements—not to be exclusive, but to ensure everyone in the room was solving similar problems at similar scales.

Today

Boston E-Com Collective is now 120+ founders representing over $85M in combined revenue. We've helped members navigate acquisition offers, survive supply chain disasters, and celebrate hitting milestones they once thought impossible.

But the core hasn't changed. It's still that same energy from the original group text: founders who show up for each other, share what's actually working, and make the lonely journey of entrepreneurship a little less lonely.

What We Believe

Givers Win

The members who get the most are the ones who give the most. We optimize for generosity, not extraction.

Small Rooms > Big Conferences

Transformation happens in intimate settings. We keep groups small so real conversations can happen.

Local Matters

Online communities are great. But there's no substitute for grabbing a drink with someone who gets it.

Honesty Over Performance

We don't need your highlight reel. We need your real numbers, real struggles, and real questions.
